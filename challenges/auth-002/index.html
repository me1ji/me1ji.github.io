Head Hunt

Author: Meiji · Category: OSINT · Difficulty: Easy


Challenge Description

It seems like the last challenge was pretty easy for you great job detective now I’ve scattered the flag across my digital footprint in the web, can you conduct a full Head Hunt to piece together their complete flag.

Try retracting your steps by looking into a professional network, word on the street is I’m polishing up my resume for recruiters.

You’ll also want to track my training habits. I spend a lot of time deploying virtual machines and conquering rooms; see if you can try to find what they are hacking on.

Finally, you know that I love free stuff and open-source. I’m currently working on a important project pushed in a special place. You might need to inspect the colors closely to get the whole profile.

Good luck, agent. Leave no stone unturned, and remember that sometimes the smallest details right in front of you hold the answers you seek. 🕵️‍♂️
